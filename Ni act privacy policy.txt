Privacy Policy — NI Act 1881 Legal Calculator

**Last updated:** 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

NI Act 1881 Legal Calculator respects your privacy. This application helps users calculate dates and limitation periods related to Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

2. Information Collection

The app is designed to calculate results using dates and information entered by the user. It does not intentionally collect or store the information entered into the calculator.

3. Data Storage

The app does not maintain a database or save users' entered calculation data on a server. Entered information is used to perform calculations and display results.

4. Sharing of Information

The app does not intentionally sell, share, or disclose users' entered calculation information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not intentionally use advertising or analytics services to collect user information. If third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated as necessary.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Security

Because the app is designed not to store entered calculation data, it does not maintain a server-side database of that information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on the Privacy Policy webpage.
